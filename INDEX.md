## 📋 The 8 Rules At A Glance

| # | Rule | Why | Where |
|---|------|-----|-------|
| 1 | No Task Without Deadline | Prevents "someday" tasks | ENFORCEMENT-RULES.md § Rule 1 |
| 2 | No Snooze Button | Stops procrastination | ENFORCEMENT-RULES.md § Rule 2 |
| 3 | No Vague Completion | Prevents hidden work | ENFORCEMENT-RULES.md § Rule 3 |
| 4 | No Indefinite Blocks | Forces problem visibility | ENFORCEMENT-RULES.md § Rule 4 |
| 5 | No Task Dumping | Enforces focus | ENFORCEMENT-RULES.md § Rule 5 |
| 6 | No Estimate Lies | Improves planning | ENFORCEMENT-RULES.md § Rule 6 |
| 7 | Weekly Report Mandatory | Creates accountability | ENFORCEMENT-RULES.md § Rule 7 |
| 8 | Escalation Rule | Prevents neglect | ENFORCEMENT-RULES.md § Rule 8 |
